CHOOSING A TENT
----------------

Solo or duo use — Alpine Pro Tent 2P (€349.99):
The Alpine Pro Tent 2P is our go-to for backpackers and hikers. At 2.10 kg, it is light enough for multi-day treks. The double-wall design separates the breathable inner from the waterproof fly, reducing condensation. Two vestibules provide gear storage so muddy boots stay outside the sleeping area.

Key specifications:
- Capacity: 2 persons
- Season rating: 3-season (spring through fall)
- Fly waterproofness: 3000 mm hydrostatic head
- Floor waterproofness: 5000 mm
- Pole material: DAC Aluminum (hubbed cross design)
- Fly fabric: 20D ripstop nylon
- Floor fabric: 40D ripstop nylon
- Packed size: 45 × 15 cm
- Doors: 2
- Vestibules: 2

Best for: backpacking, trekking, bikepacking, solo hikers who want room for gear.

Family or group camping — Alpine Pro Tent 4P (€499.00):
The 4P version shares the same proven design and waterproof ratings but offers significantly more interior space. At 3.40 kg it is heavier, but the weight can be split between two people. Color-coded DAC poles make setup intuitive — even in the dark.

Key specifications:
- Capacity: 4 persons
- Season rating: 3-season
- Fly waterproofness: 3000 mm
- Floor waterproofness: 5000 mm
- Pole material: DAC Aluminum
- Packed size: 55 × 20 cm
- Doors: 2
- Vestibules: 2

Best for: car camping, family weekends, group trips where weight per person is less critical.

WHEN TO CHOOSE 2P VS. 4P
--------------------------
- If you are backpacking solo or as a pair and weight matters, choose the 2P.
- If you are driving to a campsite or want a comfortable margin for two adults plus gear, choose the 4P.
- Both tents share the same 3000 mm fly / 5000 mm floor ratings, so waterproof protection is identical.

SEASON RATINGS EXPLAINED
--------------------------
- 3-season: Designed for spring, summer, and fall. Mesh panels provide ventilation in warm conditions. Not designed for heavy snow loads or sustained winter storms. Both Alpine Pro tents are 3-season.
- 3+ season: A 3-season tent reinforced with additional pole configurations for shoulder-season use (late autumn, early spring with light snow). We do not currently sell a 3+ model.
- 4-season: Built for winter — more poles, stronger fabrics, snow valance. We do not currently sell a 4-season tent. If you need winter tent performance, consider a specialized expedition tent from another brand and pair it with our winter-rated sleeping bag.

CHOOSING A SLEEPING BAG
-------------------------

For cold conditions — Expedition Sleeping Bag -15 °C (€299.50):
This is our premium sleeping bag. The 800-fill-power RDS goose down provides exceptional warmth-to-weight ratio. The mummy shape minimizes dead air space, keeping you warmer. An anti-snag zipper with insulated draft tube prevents cold spots along the zip line.

Key specifications:
- Fill: 800 cuin RDS goose down
- Comfort temperature: -5 °C
- Extreme temperature: -15 °C (survival only — not comfortable sleep)
- Shape: Mummy
- Shell fabric: 15D Pertex Quantum
- Packed size: 22 × 40 cm
- Weight: 1.20 kg

Best for: Alpine camping, shoulder-season treks (October–April in the Alps), high-altitude bivouacs.

Temperature rating note: The "comfort" rating (-5 °C) is the temperature at which a standard adult sleeps comfortably, per EN/ISO 23537 testing. The "extreme" rating (-15 °C) is a survival temperature — you will be cold but alive. Always choose a bag based on the comfort rating, not the extreme.

Key specifications:

CHOOSING A SLEEPING PAD
-------------------------

UltraLight Sleeping Pad (€129.00):
For any backpacking trip, a sleeping pad is essential — it provides insulation from the ground (more important than the bag for warmth) and comfort. Our pad has an R-value of 4.2, making it suitable for 3-season use and even mild winter conditions.

Key specifications:
- R-value: 4.2 (3–4 season)
- Thickness: 8 cm
- Inflation type: Self-inflating (open valve, pad expands; top up with 2–3 breaths)
- Packed size: 23 × 10 cm
- Weight: 0.45 kg
- Fabric: 30D ripstop nylon

What is R-value? R-value measures thermal resistance — how well the pad insulates you from the cold ground. Higher is warmer:
- R 1–2: Summer, warm conditions only
- R 3–4: 3-season use (our pad at R 4.2 fits here)
- R 5+: Winter and expedition use

CHOOSING A STOVE
-----------------

GasPro Compact Stove (€59.95):
Our canister stove is the simplest, lightest option for boiling water and cooking basic camp meals. At 95 g, it adds almost nothing to your pack. The integrated piezo ignition means no lighter needed (though always carry a backup).

Key specifications:
- Weight: 95 g
- Output: 2800 W
- Boil time (1 L): ~3 minutes
- Fuel type: Isobutane/propane threaded canister
- Ignition: Piezo
- Max pot size: 3 L

Best for: Backpacking, fast-packing, solo or duo trips. Ideal for boiling water for dehydrated meals and coffee.

Stove fuel note: A standard 230 g canister provides roughly 60 minutes of burn time, enough for approximately 15–20 boils of 500 ml. For a week-long trek, carry two canisters. Canisters cannot fly in checked or carry-on luggage — buy at destination if traveling by air.
