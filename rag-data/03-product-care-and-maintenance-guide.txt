OUTLANDER GEAR CO. — PRODUCT CARE AND MAINTENANCE GUIDE
=========================================================

This guide covers care instructions for every product category in our catalog. Proper maintenance extends product life far beyond the 2-year EU warranty and ensures peak performance.

TENTS — Alpine Pro Tent 2P and Alpine Pro Tent 4P
---------------------------------------------------
After each trip:
- Shake out dirt, leaves, and debris.
- If wet, set up the tent at home or hang the fly and body separately to air-dry completely before storage. Never store a tent wet — mildew will destroy the coatings.
- Wipe mud off the floor with a damp sponge.

Deep cleaning (1–2× per year):
- Set up the tent, sponge with lukewarm water and mild soap (no detergent). Rinse thoroughly.
- Never machine wash or tumble dry a tent.

DWR re-application:
- When water stops beading on the fly, re-apply a spray-on DWR treatment (Nikwax Tent & Gear SolarProof or equivalent). Follow product instructions — usually spray on, let cure 24 h.

Seam care:
- Inspect seam tape annually. If tape is peeling, apply seam sealer (Gear Aid Seam Grip) to the inside of the fly and floor.

Storage:
- Store loosely in a large cotton or mesh bag — not in the compression stuff sack. Long-term compression degrades pole shock cord and fabric coatings.
- Keep in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight.

SLEEPING BAGS — Expedition Sleeping Bag -15 °C and Summer Sleeping Bag 5 °C
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both bags use down fill (800 cuin goose and 600 cuin duck respectively).

Washing:
- Use a front-loading washing machine only (top-loaders with agitators can damage baffles).
- Wash on a gentle/delicate cycle with cold water.
- Use a down-specific detergent (Nikwax Down Wash Direct or Grangers Down Wash). Never use regular detergent — it strips the natural oils that give down its loft.
- Rinse cycle twice to remove all soap.

Drying:
- Tumble dry on low heat with 2–3 clean tennis balls. The balls break up clumps of wet down.
- This process takes 2–3 hours. Check every 30 min and break up any remaining clumps by hand.
- The bag must be 100% dry before storage. Even slight dampness causes mildew and down rot.

Storage:
- Store uncompressed in the included cotton storage sack or hang in a closet.
- Never leave in a compression stuff sack long-term.

SLEEPING PADS — UltraLight Sleeping Pad
-----------------------------------------
After each trip:
- Open the valve, roll loosely, and shake out moisture.
- Wipe with a damp cloth if dirty.

Storage:
- Store unrolled and partially inflated (valve open) to keep the foam expanded and the valve mechanism healthy.
- Avoid sharp objects near the pad.

Repair:
- Included repair kit (patch + adhesive) handles small punctures. Clean the area with alcohol, apply adhesive, press patch firmly, let cure 8 h.

STOVES — GasPro Compact Stove
-------------------------------
After each use:
- Let the stove cool completely before handling or packing.
- Wipe the burner with a dry cloth. Remove food residue from pot supports.
- Clear burner ports with the included cleaning needle if flame is uneven.

Long-term storage:
- Detach from canister. Store in a clean, dry bag.
- Check the piezo igniter before each trip — carry a backup lighter as a precaution.

Canister safety:
- Never puncture, incinerate, or store canisters above 50 °C.
- Dispose of empty canisters at proper recycling points.

CLOTHING — StormShield Jacket, Down Jacket 700, Convertible Trek Pants, Merino Base Layer Top
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hardshell (StormShield Waterproof Jacket):
- Machine wash on 30 °C gentle cycle. Use a technical fabric wash (Nikwax Tech Wash).
- Do NOT use regular detergent or fabric softener — they clog the membrane pores.
- Tumble dry on low heat for 20 min to reactivate DWR coating.
- If water no longer beads after drying, apply Nikwax TX.Direct spray or wash-in treatment.
- Close all zippers and Velcro before washing.

Down jacket (Down Jacket 700 Fill):
- Same as sleeping bag: front-loader, down-specific detergent, tumble dry with tennis balls.
- Store loosely — not compressed.

Convertible Trek Pants:
- Machine wash on 30 °C. Zip the leg sections on before washing to protect the zippers.
- Hang dry or tumble dry on low.
- If DWR fades, spray with DWR re-proofer and tumble dry 20 min on low to activate.

Merino Base Layer Top:
- Machine wash on 30 °C wool cycle or hand-wash.
- Use a mild detergent or wool-specific wash.
- Do NOT tumble dry — lay flat to dry to prevent shrinkage.
- Merino naturally resists odors; you can often wear it multiple days between washes.

FOOTWEAR — Trail Grip X, Alpinist Boots, Mid Hiking Boots, Recovery Camp Sandals
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After each use:
- Remove insoles, loosen laces, and air-dry at room temperature. Never use a dryer or direct heat source (radiator, campfire) — heat degrades adhesives and warps soles.
- Brush off dried mud with a stiff brush.

Waterproof membrane care:
- Clean with lukewarm water and a brush. When the external DWR fades (water stops beading), apply a spray-on footwear waterproofer (Nikwax Fabric & Leather Proof or Grangers Footwear Repel).
- For the Alpinist Mountaineering Boots' leather upper, periodically apply a leather conditioner to prevent cracking.

Storage:
- Store in a cool, dry place. Stuff with newspaper to maintain shape and absorb residual moisture.
- Do not store in airtight bags or car trunks (heat damage).

Recovery Camp Sandals:
- Rinse under running water. Air-dry. EVA is nearly indestructible — minimal care needed.

ACCESSORIES — Headlamp, Multi-Tool Knife, Sighting Compass Pro
----------------------------------------------------------------

Headlamp 600 Lumens:
- Wipe the lens and housing with a damp cloth.
- Charge via USB-C before long storage. Store with battery at ~50% charge.
- Remove battery if storing for more than 3 months to prevent slow discharge damage.
- IPX6 rated — resist powerful water jets. Do not submerge.

Multi-Tool Knife:
- Wipe blade dry after use. Apply a drop of machine oil to the pivot joint every few months.
- Do not put in the dishwasher. Hand-wash and dry immediately.
- Sharpen the blade with a fine whetstone or ceramic rod when it no longer slices paper cleanly.

Sighting Compass Pro:
- Keep away from magnets, speakers, and electronics that generate magnetic fields.
- Wipe the mirror with a soft cloth.
- No calibration needed — the oil-damped needle is factory-calibrated.

CLIMBING GEAR — ConfortPro Harness, Dynamic Rope 70 m, Climbing Helmet AirFlow
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Harness (ConfortPro Climbing Harness):
- Inspect before every use: check belay loop, tie-in points, buckles, and stitching for wear, cuts, or fraying.
- Wash with lukewarm water and mild soap. Hang dry away from direct sunlight.
- Retire after: a major fall (factor 1.5+), visible damage or fraying, or 3–5 years of regular use (7–10 years if used occasionally). When in doubt, retire.

Rope (Dynamic Rope 70 m × 9.5 mm):
- After each use, flake the rope loosely (do not coil tightly when wet).
- Wash in a bathtub with lukewarm water if dirty. Do not use soap.
- Dry in the shade — UV degrades nylon over time.
- Inspect the entire length by running it through your hands. Retire if: you feel lumps, flat spots, or stiff sections; the sheath is visibly damaged; or the rope has held a severe fall.
- Typical lifespan: 2–5 years of regular use, up to 10 years if used rarely and stored well.

Helmet (Climbing Helmet AirFlow):
- Inspect the shell for cracks or dents before each use.
- Replace immediately after any significant impact, even if no visible damage.
- Wash with mild soap and water. Dry in the shade.
- Replace every 5 years regardless of visible condition (material degradation).

HYDRATION — Insulated Bottle, MiniPure Filter, Hydration Bladder 3 L
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Insulated Bottle 1 L:
- Hand-wash with warm water and bottle-cleaning brush. For stubborn odors, soak with a baking soda solution (1 tbsp per 500 ml) overnight.
- Do not microwave or freeze (vacuum insulation can be damaged by extreme thermal stress).
- The exterior can be wiped with a damp cloth.

MiniPure Water Filter:
- After each trip, backflush the filter by blowing clean water (or air) in reverse through the outlet.
- If flow rate drops significantly, soak in clean water for 15 min and backflush again.
- Never allow the filter to freeze while wet — ice crystals destroy the hollow-fiber membrane. If the filter may have frozen, replace it.
- Store with caps off in a cool, dry place.

Hydration Bladder 3 L:
- After each use, rinse with warm water. For deep cleaning, use a bladder-cleaning tablet or diluted baking soda solution.
- Hang upside-down with the opening wide to dry completely.
- Store with the opening propped open (a rolled-up towel inside works well) to prevent mold.
- Never freeze the bladder when full — the seam welds can rupture.

WINTER SPORTS — Alpine Ski Goggles, Insulated Mountain Gloves
---------------------------------------------------------------

Ski Goggles:
- Never wipe the inside of the lens with a cloth — this damages the anti-fog coating. Let moisture evaporate naturally or use a gentle blower.
- Store in the included microfiber pouch to prevent scratches.
- Keep away from direct heat sources.

Insulated Mountain Gloves:
- Hand-wash in lukewarm water with mild soap. Gently squeeze out water (do not wring leather).
- Dry at room temperature, away from heat. Stuff loosely with paper to maintain shape.
- Re-apply leather conditioner to the palms once per season.

TRAVEL & PACKING — Packing Cube Set, Microfiber Travel Towel
--------------------------------------------------------------

Packing Cubes:
- Machine wash on cold, gentle cycle. Air dry. Ripstop nylon is rugged — these cubes will outlast most luggage.

Microfiber Travel Towel:
- Machine wash on 30 °C. No fabric softener (it reduces absorption).
- Air dry or tumble dry on low. Dries very quickly on its own.

GENERAL WARRANTY INFORMATION
-----------------------------
All Outlander Gear Co. products carry the EU-mandated 2-year legal warranty against manufacturing defects. This covers:
- Seam failures under normal use
- Zipper defects
- Delamination of waterproof membranes unrelated to wear
- Hardware failure (buckles, clips, hooks)

Not covered: normal wear, cuts, burns, UV degradation from extended storage in sunlight, damage from improper washing, or misuse.
