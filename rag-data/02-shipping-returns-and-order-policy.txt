OUTLANDER GEAR CO. — SHIPPING, RETURNS, AND ORDER POLICIES
============================================================

SHIPPING OVERVIEW
-----------------
Outlander Gear Co. ships from our warehouse in Annecy, France. We offer three shipping tiers within France and the European Union. All orders are processed Monday through Friday; orders placed before 2:00 PM CET on a business day ship the same day.

FREE SHIPPING THRESHOLD
------------------------
Orders totaling €100 or more qualify for free Standard shipping within France and the EU. This applies automatically at checkout — no promo code required.

SHIPPING TO SWITZERLAND, NORWAY, AND UK
-----------------------------------------
These countries may incur customs duties and import taxes upon delivery. Outlander Gear Co. does not collect duties at checkout. Customers are responsible for any charges assessed by their country's customs authority. Typical duties for outdoor gear range from 0–12% depending on the product classification.

ORDER PROCESSING
-----------------
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM CET (Monday–Friday) ship the same business day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM CET or on weekends/holidays ship the next business day.
- French public holidays: offices closed, no shipments.
- You receive a confirmation email with a tracking number once your parcel ships.

HOLIDAY SHIPPING DEADLINES (APPROXIMATE)
-----------------------------------------
For guaranteed Christmas delivery within France:
- Standard: order by December 17
- Express: order by December 21
For EU destinations, allow 2–3 additional business days.

ORDER MODIFICATION AND CANCELLATION
-------------------------------------
- You may modify or cancel an order only while its status is "Pending."
- Once the status changes to "Processing," the order is being packed and cannot be modified.
- To request a modification, email support@outlander-gear.co with your order number immediately.

ORDER STATUSES
--------------
| Status      | Meaning                                                |
|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------|

| Confirmed   | Payment verified, warehouse notified                   |

RETURNS POLICY — 30 DAYS
--------------------------
We accept returns within 30 calendar days of delivery. To be eligible:
1. The item must be unused, unworn, and in its original condition.
2. All tags, labels, and original packaging must be included.
3. You must initiate the return by emailing support@outlander-gear.co with your order number and reason.

We will reply within 24 hours with a return shipping label (prepaid for defective items; at customer cost for change-of-mind returns within France — €6.95 flat rate deducted from refund).

RETURN PROCESS — STEP BY STEP
-------------------------------
1. Email support@outlander-gear.co with order number, item(s) to return, and reason.
2. Receive a return authorization and shipping label within 24 hours.
3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging.
4. Drop off at a Colissimo or Mondial Relay point (or schedule carrier pickup for Express returns).
5. Once we receive and inspect the item (1–3 business days), your refund is issued.
6. Refund appears on your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

HYGIENE EXCEPTIONS
-------------------
For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned if opened or used:
- MiniPure Water Filter (once the seal is broken or the filter has been exposed to water)
- Hydration Bladder 3 L (if the bladder has been filled)
- Merino Base Layer Top (if worn without tags)

These items may still be returned if sealed and unused.

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
----------------------------
If your item arrives damaged or has a manufacturing defect:
1. Report it within 48 hours of delivery by emailing support@outlander-gear.co with photos.
2. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including original shipping costs.
3. You do not need to return the damaged item unless we specifically request it.

EU WARRANTY
-----------
Under EU consumer law, all products sold by Outlander Gear Co. carry a minimum 2-year legal warranty against manufacturing defects. This covers defects in materials or workmanship that appear under normal use. It does not cover wear and tear, misuse, or damage from accidents.

PAYMENT METHODS
----------------
- Credit/debit card: Visa, Mastercard, American Express (Stripe-powered, PCI-DSS compliant)
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay (on supported devices)
- Bank transfer: available for orders over €500 (contact support for details)

All prices on the website are in euros (€) and include applicable VAT for EU customers.

GIFT ORDERS
-----------
We do not currently offer gift wrapping, but you can add a personal note in the order "Notes" field at checkout. The packing slip does not show prices when a note is included — useful for gifts.

BULK / CORPORATE ORDERS
-------------------------
For orders of 10+ units (e.g., team gear for a club or corporate retreat), contact support@outlander-gear.co for a custom quote. We offer volume discounts of 10–15% depending on order size and product mix.
