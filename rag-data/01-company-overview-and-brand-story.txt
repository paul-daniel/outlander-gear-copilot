OUTLANDER GEAR CO. — COMPANY OVERVIEW AND BRAND STORY
======================================================

ABOUT US
--------
Outlander Gear Co. is a European outdoor equipment company headquartered in Annecy, France, at the foot of the French Alps. Founded in 2019 by a team of mountaineers, trail runners, and expedition leaders, our mission is simple: make the outdoors accessible, safe, and enjoyable for everyone — from first-time campers to seasoned alpinists.

We design, test, and curate a focused catalog of 31 products across 9 categories. Every item in our store has been field-tested in the Alps, Pyrenees, Corsica, and Scandinavia before earning a place in our collection. We do not chase trends or stock hundreds of mediocre items. We believe a smaller, carefully chosen lineup means better quality control, deeper product knowledge, and more honest recommendations.

OUR FOUNDING STORY
------------------
The idea for Outlander Gear Co. was born during a stormy bivouac on the Tour du Mont Blanc in 2018. Three friends — an engineer, a mountain guide, and a product designer — found themselves debating which pieces of gear actually mattered versus the marketing noise that clutters the outdoor industry. They agreed on a principle: if you cannot explain why a product is worth carrying up a mountain, it should not be in your pack.

By 2019, the first Outlander Gear Co. website went live with just 12 products. Every item was hand-picked, field-tested, and backed by transparent specifications. No vague "premium quality" labels — just real numbers: hydrostatic head in millimeters, fill power in cubic inches, R-values, UIAA fall ratings, and Vibram sole models.

Today our catalog has grown to 31 products across 9 categories, but the philosophy has not changed. We add a product only when it fills a genuine gap and passes our testing protocol.

SUSTAINABILITY COMMITMENTS
--------------------------
- Down sourcing: All down products (Expedition Sleeping Bag -15 °C, Summer Sleeping Bag 5 °C, Down Jacket 700 Fill) use RDS (Responsible Down Standard) certified down, ensuring ethical treatment of waterfowl.
- Fabric choices: Our shells and tent fabrics are bluesign approved, meaning they are produced with reduced impact on people and the environment.
- Durability over disposability: We design products to last years, not seasons. Our 2-year EU legal warranty covers manufacturing defects, and our care guides help customers extend product life well beyond that.
- Packaging: We ship in recycled cardboard with paper padding — no single-use plastic fillers.

Our Product Range

Outlander Gear Co. offers a carefully curated selection of outdoor gear across seven main categories:

Camping — Our camping collection includes lightweight tents, expedition-grade sleeping bags, ultralight sleeping pads, and compact stoves. These products are designed for multi-season use, from spring through autumn, and some are rated for extreme winter conditions. Our flagship products include the Alpine Pro 3-Season Tent (a 2-person tent with 3000mm waterproof rating and DAC aluminum poles) and the Expedition Sleeping Bag rated to -15°C with 800-fill-power goose down.

Hiking — For day hikes to multi-week treks, we offer ergonomic backpacks (from 12L trail running vests to 55L trekking packs), carbon fiber trekking poles, and essential accessories. Our hiking packs feature internal aluminum frames, padded hip belts, and integrated rain covers to keep your gear dry in any conditions.

Clothing — Our apparel line focuses on technical outdoor clothing built for performance in the elements. This includes 3-layer hardshell jackets with sealed seams and 20,000 g/m²/24h breathability, compressible 700-fill-power down jackets, and convertible trek pants with UV50+ protection. Every garment is designed using the layering system principle — base layer, mid layer, and outer shell — so customers can mix and match for any weather condition.

Footwear — From trail running shoes to high-altitude mountaineering boots, our footwear selection covers every type of terrain. We carry shoes with Vibram Megagrip outsoles for maximum traction, Gore-Tex Insulated membranes for warmth and waterproofing, and Thinsulate insulation for extreme cold. We also offer recovery sandals for post-hike comfort at camp.

Accessories — Essential gear that makes a difference in the field: rechargeable USB-C headlamps with up to 600 lumens output, multi-function folding knives, and professional-grade sighting compasses with topographic scales. These are the small items that can turn a good trip into a great one.

Climbing — For rock climbing and mountaineering, we offer CE-certified harnesses, dynamic climbing ropes with dry treatment for wet conditions, and other essential safety equipment. All our climbing gear meets or exceeds European safety standards (EN 12277 for harnesses, UIAA certifications for ropes).

Hydration — Staying hydrated is critical in the outdoors. Our hydration lineup includes double-wall stainless steel insulated bottles that keep drinks cold for 24 hours or hot for 12 hours, as well as portable water filtration systems capable of removing 99.9999% of bacteria and 99.9% of protozoa from natural water sources.
