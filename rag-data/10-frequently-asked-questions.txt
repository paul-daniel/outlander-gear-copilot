OUTLANDER GEAR CO. — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
=================================================

ORDERING AND ACCOUNT
----------------------

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click "Sign Up" on our website, enter your email, and choose a password. You will receive a confirmation email. Once verified, you can start shopping.

Q: Can I order without creating an account?
A: No, an account is required to place orders. This lets you track your orders, save addresses, and manage your wishlist.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and bank transfer (SEPA for EU customers).

Q: Are prices shown in euros?
A: Yes, all prices on our website are in euros (€) and include VAT for EU customers.

Q: Can I change or cancel my order?
A: You can cancel within 1 hour of placing the order by contacting support@outlandergear.co. After that, we begin warehouse processing and cannot guarantee changes.

SHIPPING
---------

Q: Where do you ship?
A: We ship to all EU countries, Switzerland, Norway, the UK, the US, and Canada.

Q: How much does shipping cost?
A: Standard shipping is €5.99 within the EU and €12.99 for international orders. Orders over €100 ship free within the EU.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: EU orders: 3–5 business days. UK/Switzerland/Norway: 5–7 business days. US/Canada: 7–14 business days.

Q: Do you offer express shipping?
A: Yes. Express shipping (1–2 business days within the EU) is available for €14.99.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once shipped, you will receive a tracking number by email. You can also view tracking status in your account under "My Orders."

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES
----------------------

Q: What is your return policy?
A: You have 30 days from delivery to return unused items in original packaging for a full refund. Items must have all tags attached and show no signs of use. Climbing safety gear (harnesses, ropes, helmets) cannot be returned once the sealed safety packaging is opened, due to safety certification requirements.

Q: How do I start a return?
A: Log in to your account, go to "My Orders," select the order, and click "Request Return." You will receive a prepaid return label by email.

Q: Who pays for return shipping?
A: We provide a free return label for EU customers. International returns are at the customer's expense.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. The refund goes back to your original payment method.

Q: Can I exchange an item for a different size?
A: Yes. Request a return for the original item and place a new order for the correct size. This is faster than waiting for an exchange.

Q: What temperature is the Expedition Sleeping Bag rated to?

PRODUCTS — FOOTWEAR
---------------------

Q: What is the difference between the Trail Grip X and Mid Hiking Boots?
A: The Trail Grip X (€159.99) is a low-cut trail shoe weighing 310 g per shoe — fast, agile, not waterproof, best for trail running and light hikes. The Mid Hiking Boots (€169.00) are mid-cut with a waterproof membrane, weighing 460 g per shoe — better ankle support and weather protection, ideal for varied terrain and moderate loads.

Q: Are the Alpinist Boots compatible with crampons?
A: Yes, the Alpinist Boots (€389.00) are B2-rated and compatible with strap-on and semi-automatic crampons. They are NOT compatible with step-in (C3) crampons.

Q: How do I break in new hiking boots?
A: Wear them around the house for a few hours, then on short walks before taking them on a full hike. Our Mid Hiking Boots use Nubuck leather that softens quickly.

Q: How should I size my trail shoes?
A: Measure your feet in the afternoon when they are largest. Wear the socks you will hike in. Leave about one finger width between your longest toe and the front of the shoe. Go half a size up if in doubt — your feet swell during long hikes.

PRODUCTS — CLIMBING
---------------------

Q: Is the ComfortPro Harness suitable for beginners?
A: Yes, the ComfortPro Harness (€79.95) is an all-round harness suitable for sport climbing, trad climbing, and indoor climbing. Its adjustable waist (68–120 cm) and leg loops fit a wide range of body shapes.

Q: How long does a climbing rope last?
A: With regular weekend use (100+ days per year), retire after 3–5 years. With occasional use (20–50 days per year), 5–7 years. Maximum lifespan is 10 years from manufacture date regardless of use. Our Dynamic Rope 70 m (€189.00) meets EN 892 and UIAA standards.

Q: Do I need a helmet for indoor climbing?
A: Indoor gyms typically do not require helmets because there is no rockfall risk. However, for all outdoor climbing, we strongly recommend our Climbing Helmet (€69.00).
