=== PRODUCT CARE ===

Q: How do I wash a down jacket or sleeping bag?
A: Use a front-loading washing machine on a gentle cycle with a down-specific detergent (like Nikwax Down Wash Direct). Tumble dry on low heat with 2–3 clean tennis balls to restore loft. Never use regular detergent or fabric softener. See our Product Care Guide for detailed instructions.

Q: How do I maintain the waterproofing on my jacket?
A: Wash with a technical fabric wash (Nikwax Tech Wash), then tumble dry on low heat for 20 minutes to reactivate the DWR coating. If water no longer beads on the surface, apply a DWR reproofing treatment (Nikwax TX.Direct). See our Product Care Guide.

Q: How do I clean my tent?
A: Never machine wash a tent. Set it up and wipe with a damp sponge or use a tent-specific cleaner. Always dry completely before storage. Store loosely in a breathable bag, not in the tight stuff sack. See our Product Care Guide.

Q: When should I retire my climbing harness or rope?
A: Harnesses: 3–5 years with regular use, 7–10 years with occasional use. Ropes: 2–5 years with regular use, 10 years maximum. Retire immediately if you see visible damage (fraying, cuts, core exposure). See our Climbing Safety Guide.

=== WARRANTY ===

Q: Do your products come with a warranty?
A: All products sold by Outlander Gear Co. are covered by the standard EU legal warranty (2 years) for manufacturing defects. Some products may have additional manufacturer warranties.
