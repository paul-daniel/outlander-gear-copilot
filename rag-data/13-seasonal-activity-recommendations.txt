Recommended activities: Day hiking, early-season camping, trail running.

- StormShield Waterproof Jacket (€259.00) — Spring rain is guaranteed. A reliable shell is your most important spring layer.
- Convertible Trek Pants (€89.00) — Start in long pants for cool mornings; zip off the legs when it warms up.
- Merino Base Layer Top (€79.00) — Regulates temperature across the day's wide range.
- Mid Hiking Boots (€169.00) — Waterproof protection for muddy, wet trails.
- Day Hike Pack 28L (€89.95) — Enough space for layers, food, and water.
- MiniPure Water Filter (€44.95) — Snowmelt streams are flowing; refill on the trail.
- Headlamp 600 Lumens (€49.99) — Days are getting longer but carry one for unexpected delays.

AUTUMN (September – November)
-------------------------------
Conditions: Cooling temperatures, shorter days, early frost, beautiful foliage, quieter trails.

Recommended activities: Hiking (peak season for many regions), camping, photography trips.

Suggested gear:
- Down Jacket 700 Fill (€179.00) — Mornings and evenings get cold fast. Pack it as a mid-layer or standalone rest stop layer.
- StormShield Waterproof Jacket (€259.00) — Autumn storms can be intense.
- Merino Base Layer Top (€79.00) — Warm base for dropping temperatures.
- Mid Hiking Boots (€169.00) — Wet leaves and muddy trails need waterproofing and grip.
- Trekking Backpack 55L (€189.95) or Day Hike Pack 28L (€89.95) — Depends on trip length.
- Headlamp 600 Lumens (€49.99) — Shorter days mean more chance of hiking in the dark.
- Insulated Gloves (€79.00) — Cold hands are the first sign of dropping temps. Have gloves ready.
- Camp Chair Ultralight (€69.95) — Autumn camp evenings are worth sitting around for.

Autumn weekend camping kit: Tent + bag + pad + stove + warm layers ≈ €1,200–€1,500

- Ski Goggles Pro (€89.00) — Anti-fog, contrast-enhancing lens for flat light on snow.
- Down Jacket 700 Fill (€179.00) — Core insulation layer.
- StormShield Waterproof Jacket (€259.00) — Outer shell against wind and snow.
- Merino Base Layer Top (€79.00) — Moisture-wicking warm base.
- Expedition Sleeping Bag −15 °C (€299.50) — For winter camping or hut emergencies.
- Headlamp 600 Lumens (€49.99) — Sunrise at 8 AM, sunset at 4 PM — you will need light.

Winter mountaineering day pack:
- Day Hike Pack 28L (€89.95)
- Down Jacket 700 Fill, StormShield Jacket, Merino Base Layer
- Insulated Gloves, Ski Goggles
- Headlamp, Insulated Bottle (keeps water from freezing)

Winter safety note: Always check avalanche forecasts before entering avalanche terrain. Carry avalanche safety gear (transceiver, probe, shovel — not currently in our catalog) and know how to use it.
