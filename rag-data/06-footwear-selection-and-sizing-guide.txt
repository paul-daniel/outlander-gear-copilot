HOW TO SIZE YOUR OUTDOOR FOOTWEAR
-----------------------------------
1. Measure both feet in the afternoon (feet swell during the day).
2. Wear the socks you plan to hike in (e.g., our merino blend socks).
3. Stand up and slide your index finger behind your heel — there should be roughly one finger width of space between your longest toe and the front of the boot.
4. Walk downhill (use a ramp or stairs). Your toes should NOT hit the front of the boot. If they do, go up half a size.
5. For mountaineering boots like the Alpinist Boots, size for thicker socks and slightly more room.

Common sizing mistakes:
- Buying shoes too small → black toenails on descents
- Buying boots too large → heel lift → blisters on the back of the heel
- Ignoring width → pressure points on the sides of the foot

BOOT CARE
-----------
- After each hike: Remove insoles, open laces wide, and air-dry at room temperature. Never use a direct heat source (radiators, campfires, hair dryers) — heat damages adhesives and warps midsoles.
- Cleaning: Brush off dirt with a soft brush and lukewarm water. For leather boots, apply a leather conditioner once or twice per season.
- Waterproofing: Re-apply DWR spray every 20–30 wears (or when water stops beading). For leather boots, use wax-based waterproofing.
- Storage: Store in a cool, dry place with insoles removed. Stuff with newspaper to hold shape.
