Trekking Backpack 55L (€189.95):
Our largest pack, designed for multi-day treks where you carry tent, sleeping bag, food, and cooking gear. The internal aluminum frame transfers load to the padded hip belt, keeping weight off your shoulders on long descents. Key features:
- Volume: 55 L (expandable lid adds ~5 L)
- Frame: Internal aluminum
- Rain cover: Integrated, stows in bottom pocket
- Access: U-zip front panel + top lid
- Hydration: Internal sleeve fits bladders up to 3 L
- Torso range: Adjustable 42–52 cm
- Hip belt: Padded, with zippered hip pockets
- Weight: 1.80 kg

Best for: GR routes, multi-day Pyrenees/Alps treks, backpacking in Scandinavia, anywhere you need to be self-sufficient for 3+ days.

Fit tip: Measure your torso length (C7 vertebra to iliac crest). Adjust the pack's torso strap so the hip belt sits on top of your hip bones, not your waist. When loaded, the shoulder straps should wrap over your shoulders with no gap, and load-lifter straps should angle back at roughly 45°.

TREKKING POLES — CARBON TREKKING POLES (€89.95)
-------------------------------------------------
Trekking poles reduce knee impact by 20–25% on descents and provide stability on rough terrain. Our carbon poles weigh just 380 g for the pair — about half the weight of aluminum equivalents.

Key specifications:
- Material: Carbon fiber shaft
- Grip: Cork (absorbs moisture, molds to hand over time)
- Tip: Tungsten carbide (grips on rock and ice)
- Length range: 65–135 cm (adjustable via FlickLock)
- Lock system: FlickLock Pro (reliable in all conditions)
- Included: Rubber tips (for pavement), snow baskets

When to use poles:
- Steep descents: Poles absorb shock and protect your knees.
- River crossings: Two extra contact points improve stability.
- Carrying heavy loads: Poles distribute load and improve posture.
- After injury: Poles reduce strain on recovering joints.

Carbon vs. aluminum: Carbon is lighter and absorbs vibration better. Aluminum is more durable if you frequently force poles between rocks. Our Carbon Trekking Poles are ideal for hikers who prioritize low weight.

THE 3-LAYER CLOTHING SYSTEM FOR HIKERS
----------------------------------------
Every hiker should dress in three layers:

1. Base layer — Worn against the skin. Wicks moisture away from your body. Our Merino Base Layer Top (€79.00, 185 g/m² Merino wool) is naturally odor-resistant and temperature-regulating.

2. Mid layer — Traps body heat. Our Down Jacket 700 Fill (€179.00) is an excellent mid-layer: 700 cuin RDS duck down, packs into its own pocket.

3. Outer layer — Blocks wind and rain. The StormShield Waterproof Jacket (€259.00) is a 3-layer hardshell with 20,000 mm waterproofing and 20,000 g/m²/24 h breathability.

This system works for any temperature: add or remove layers as conditions change.
